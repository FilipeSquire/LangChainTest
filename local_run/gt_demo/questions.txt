How is Walmart using artificial intelligence in its business activities?

R: Walmart is leveraging artificial intelligence (AI) across various aspects of its operations. For instance, AI and generative AI (Gen AI) are used for forecasting demand at a granular level, which helps in placing inventory in the right locations. Walmart has been utilizing machine learning technology for a long time, and Gen AI enhances this by utilizing Transformer Technology for improved accuracy. Additionally, AI is employed to clean up catalog content submitted by sellers, ensuring better product matching. Overall, AI impacts customer experience, inventory management, and operational efficiency, demonstrating Walmart's commitment to integrating technology into its business activities.

Expansion of the personal shopping assistant, Sparky, which will help customers explore products and complete shopping tasks.

Enhanced use of AI tools by developers and engineers in the development lifecycle, including planning, coding, testing, and troubleshooting.
Integration of AI across various business segments and geographies to improve efficiency and reduce costs.
Overall improvements in decision-making through physical AI that mimics complex environments in stores and supply chains.
